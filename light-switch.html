Home automation

Automatic Light Switch

Counting people at the door

I lined up two ultrasonic sensors along the doorway. The Arduino used the order in which they detected someone to tell whether that person was entering or leaving. It added one to the room count on entry and subtracted one on exit, so a single person leaving wouldn’t register the room as empty while others were still inside.

Automatic shutoff and remote control

We added a sound sensor to monitor the room for activity. After a period of quiet, the system could send the servo to rotate and flip the existing light switch off. I also added a Bluetooth module so someone could turn the lights on or off remotely from bed using the same servo.
